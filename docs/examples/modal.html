Sheet modifier

.ui-modal-sheet is meant for side panels such as filters. Below 768px it is a bottom sheet with a drag handle, at most 85dvh tall, with the body scrolling between the fixed header and footer and the footer buttons sharing the width. On desktop it behaves like .ui-modal-narrow (a centered 420px modal); a right-side drawer was considered and left out to keep modal.css simple. Without the modifier, .ui-modal is unchanged.
